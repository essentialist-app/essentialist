## Basic Arithmetic and Greek Symbols

Here are simple variables, operators, and common Greek letters:

$a \times b - c + d = 0$

And basic Greek notation:

$\alpha + \beta = \gamma$
$\theta \times \phi = \psi + \omega$

## Powers, Subscripts and Roots

Basic exponents and indexes:

$x^2 + y_i^3 = z_{n,m}$

Nested exponents and nested subscripts:

$x^{a^b} + y_{i_j} = 1$

Square roots, nested square roots, and optional arguments (like cube roots):

$c = \pm\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$

$$
y = \sqrt{1 + \sqrt{x}}
$$

$$
R = \sqrt[3]{x^3 + y^3}
$$

## Fractions and Large Expressions

Fractions of varying styles:

$$
\pi = \frac{c}{d}
$$

The quadratic formula (fraction with nested roots and signs):

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

A nested, double-story fraction:

$$
F = \frac{\frac{a}{b}}{\frac{c}{d}}
$$

## Solution to the Basel Problem

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}
$$

## Product operator with limits

$$
\prod_{n=1}^{\infty} (1 - x_n)
$$

## Limit operator with subscript limit centered below

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1
$$
